Four-Step Setup

From zip to e-reader in a minute.

  1. 01

    Connect via USB

    Plug your Kindle, Kobo, or e-reader into your computer in USB mass storage mode.

  2. 02

    Unzip into plugins/

    Extract the archive into KOReader's plugins/ folder. The zip already contains tomedown.koplugin/.

  3. 03

    Restart KOReader

    Unplug your device and restart KOReader (or enable it in ☰ → Plugin management).

  4. 04

    Open Tomedown

    Find Tomedown right at the top of KOReader's menu and run your first local export.

No USB Cable?

Install straight from your e-reader.

Prefer a wireless setup? Both community plugin stores carry Tomedown, so you can install and update without touching a computer.

Storefront Wireless

Open Storefront in KOReader, search Tomedown, and tap Install.

AppStore Wireless

Browse AppStore on your device, pick Tomedown, and restart KOReader.

Good to Know

Requirements & updates

  • KOReader with modern highlights (older .sdr files work too).
  • No cloud required: local export works completely offline; WebDAV, Dropbox, and FTP are optional.
  • Future updates: after the first install, update in one tap via Settings → Updates → Check for updates.
