03 · TWO-MINUTE SETUP

Quick installation.

Up and running in two minutes. Offline by default, wireless sync whenever you want it.

1. Install via Storefront or USB

Search Tomedown directly in KOReader’s wireless Storefront / AppStore, or unpack the release archive into your device’s koreader/plugins/ directory:

unzip tomedown.koplugin-v{{version}}.zip -d /path/to/koreader/plugins/
  1. Restart KOReader after copying the tomedown.koplugin/ folder.
  2. Open ☰ → Tomedown (or assign any gesture to quick export).
  3. Tap Export this book or All books → Export changed books.

2. Open your exported library

Your book folios, cover thumbnails, stylus sketches, and library catalog are written straight to local storage:

koreader/clipboard/tomedown/
├── covers/
├── drawings/
├── 00 - Index.md
├── 00 - Reading Digest.md
└── Arthur Machen - The Three Impostors.md

Want automatic wireless delivery to your vault? Configure WebDAV, Nextcloud, Koofr, Dropbox, or SFTP in Settings → Cloud upload → Destination.

04 · FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Clear answers.

Everything you need to know before installing. Deep dives and edge cases live in the documentation.

Browse all FAQs
Do I need an account or cloud server?

No. Tomedown runs 100% offline on your e-reader and writes directly to local storage. Wireless cloud upload (WebDAV, Nextcloud, Koofr, Dropbox, FTP/SFTP) in Settings → Cloud upload is strictly optional.

Does Tomedown 3.0 work outside Obsidian?

Yes. Switch Settings → Export → Markdown style from Obsidian to Standard Markdown (or export .txt) to omit Obsidian-specific callouts, wikilinks, and ^hl- block IDs—producing pure CommonMark ready for Logseq, Bear, iA Writer, Zettlr, or Git.

Which e-readers and formats are supported?

Any device running KOReader (Kindle, Kobo, PocketBook, Boox, Android, Linux) and any format KOReader can annotate (EPUB, PDF, Kepub, DjVu, FB2, CBZ). Written in pure Lua with zero native binary dependencies.

How are stylus handwritten notes exported?

Tomedown reads KOReader’s vector stroke data from each book’s .sdr folder and renders clean, resolution-independent drawings/*.svg files embedded right alongside the corresponding page and chapter.

Will re-exporting erase notes I wrote in my vault?

No. With Keep my notes enabled, Tomedown preserves your freeform commentary and custom YAML properties, optionally pulls back highlight edits made in Obsidian, and keeps rotating .bak safety copies.

Is Tomedown free and open source?

Yes—free software under the GNU AGPL-3.0 license, with zero telemetry, zero ads, and portable settings backup (tomedown_settings_backup.lua).
