03 · TWO-MINUTE SETUP

Quick installation.

Up and running in two minutes. Offline by default, wireless sync whenever you want it.

1. Install via USB or Storefront

Search Tomedown directly in KOReader’s wireless Storefront / AppStore, or unpack the release archive into your device’s koreader/plugins/ directory:

unzip tomedown.koplugin-v{{version}}.zip -d /path/to/koreader/plugins/
  1. Restart KOReader after copying the tomedown.koplugin/ folder.
  2. Open ☰ → Tomedown (or tap the Tomedown icon in the top bar).
  3. Tap Export this book or All books → Export changed books.

2. Find your exported notes

Your standalone book notes and library index are written immediately to local storage on your e-reader:

koreader/clipboard/tomedown/
├── 00 - Index.md
├── 00 - Reading Digest.md
└── Arthur Machen - The Three Impostors.md

Want automatic wireless delivery to Obsidian? Configure your WebDAV, Koofr, Nextcloud, or Dropbox target in Settings → Cloud upload → Destination.

04 · FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Clear answers.

Quick answers before you install. Deep dives and troubleshooting live in the documentation.

Browse all FAQs
Do I need an account or cloud server?

No. Tomedown exports directly to local storage on your e-reader. Cloud syncing (WebDAV, Dropbox, FTP) in Settings → Cloud upload is completely optional.

Which hardware and readers are supported?

Any device capable of running KOReader (Kindle, Kobo, Android, PocketBook). The plugin is written in pure Lua and does not access proprietary hardware APIs.

Where do the exported files go?

By default into clipboard/tomedown inside KOReader’s data folder (one Markdown or .txt file per book, plus 00 - Index.md). You can change the folder in Settings → Export → Local folder.

Will re-exporting erase my notes written in Obsidian?

No. With Keep my notes active, Tomedown parses existing notes, preserves personal commentary outside the highlight blocks, and maintains backup copies (.bak).

Can I customize how notes and files are named?

Yes. In Settings → Export → File names & folders, Tomedown supports custom filename templates (%A - %T) and folder organization (by author, series, or reading status).

Is Tomedown open source?

Yes, licensed under the GNU AGPLv3. No telemetry, no proprietary lock-in.
