Colophon & Story

Notes belong with the book.

Tomedown started as a quiet frustration: every volume I read in KOReader deserved its own permanent Markdown file, not an endless dump of mixed quotes.

01 · The Origin

Built for readers who keep a library, not a clipboard.

When you finish a book on an e-reader, your highlights are the map of what stayed with you. Traditional export tools flatten every volume you have ever read into a single, unwieldy file.

I built Tomedown so every book gets its own clean note, complete with YAML frontmatter, tags, reading progress, and a navigable library index. It has been in daily use since version 1.0, and almost everything added since (from Keep my notes to the chronological Reading Digest and clean tag pills) came directly from reading real books.

02 · The Philosophy

Notes as literature. Code as craftsmanship.

Three convictions that define how Tomedown is designed, built, and maintained.

Pillar 01 Longevity

Plain files outlive walled gardens

Platforms rise and decay; proprietary cloud databases vanish when business models pivot. Standard Markdown is universal, human-readable, and timeless. Notes written today will still open fifty years from now on any operating system.

.md · .txt · zero lock-in
Pillar 02 Sovereignty

The reader’s voice is inviolable

Software must respect the reader’s intellectual work. When you write commentary or synthesis in your vault, Tomedown treats your words as sacred: subsequent exports fold new quotes in without ever altering a syllable of your writing.

Keep my notes · cryptographic hashes · automatic .bak
Pillar 03 Independence

Frugal, local, quiet

No telemetry, no analytics, no mandatory accounts. Tomedown executes entirely inside KOReader using pure Lua, drawing minimal battery and emitting zero network packets unless you explicitly choose to bridge your notes to the cloud.

100% offline · pure Lua · GNU AGPL-3.0
03 · The Visual Identity

The craft behind the mark.

Every curve, shade, and line in Tomedown’s identity is born from the tactile reality of physical books and e-ink reading.

Master Mark · v6

Layered Pages

Three stacked page edges (#f4ecd8 / #b8aa85) evoke the physical thickness of a volume and the tangible passage of chapters.

Golden Ribbon

The swallowtail bookmark ribbon (#f8c82e) holds your place, representing memory and personal reading progress.

Markdown Arrow

The calligraphic down-arrow reflects Markdown notation and the direct transfer of knowledge from your e-reader into plain files.

Amber Highlight

The bold yellow underline (#f0b00a) mirrors the physical felt-tip stroke across meaningful sentences.
