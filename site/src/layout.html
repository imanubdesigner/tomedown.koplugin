tomedown

Open-source KOReader plugin that turns your e-reader highlights, personal notes, and reading progress into clean, self-contained Markdown files for Obsidian, Logseq, or any plain text editor.

Free software under the AGPL-3.0 license. No cookies, no analytics, no tracking on this site, and 100% offline-first execution on your device.
